About

I'm a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I'm also completing an MS in Mathematics. I'm advised by Prof. Srinivasa M. Salapaka, and my research is on optimization, planning and scheduling over large-scale networks: multi-agent routing, facility location and constrained resource allocation. My tools come from the Maximum Entropy Principle, control barrier functions, reinforcement learning and online learning, and I build scalable computational frameworks for them in Python and PyTorch. I also work with Prof. Ankur Mani on distributed online stochastic optimization with bandit feedback.

Before Illinois, I completed a dual B.Tech + M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay with a minor in Systems and Control Engineering, advised by Prof. Ravi N. Banavar. There I worked on geometric mechanics and discrete geometric control of flexible-link and multi-DOF robot manipulators, and on energy optimisation in wave energy converters, including a collaboration with Dr. Paul Kotyczka at TU Munich.

Research

Optimization and Planning · UIUC, with Prof. Srinivasa M. Salapaka and Prof. Ankur Mani

Distributed Online Stochastic Linear Optimization

Oct 2025 – Feb 2026 · Under review, ACM EC 2026
  • Formulated an online stochastic linear optimization framework with bandit feedback, in which myopic agents are guided by a central planner.
  • Designed a subspace contraction policy that lets the planner steer agents toward the optimal decision.
  • Proved problem-independent regret bounds of O(d3.5 log T) for a general polytope decision space.

Simultaneous Multi-Agent Routing and Scheduling

Jan 2024 – present · AIAA SciTech 2025
  • Built a modular Python framework for conflict-free multi-agent routing and scheduling on graph networks, optimizing makespan and total travel cost across large networks.
  • Achieved 10× faster runtime than a penalty-based baseline with an annealing-inspired optimization algorithm integrated with control barrier functions.

Parametrized Multi-Agent Routing via Deep Attention Models

Mar 2025 – Aug 2025 · arXiv
  • Designed a hybrid shortest-path and uniform-path sampling scheme for multi-agent routing and facility location, scaling up to 10× beyond baselines at comparable cost and faster runtimes.
  • Accelerated gradient-based optimization by O(M2), with 10× lower costs than existing metaheuristics and a 1500× speedup over Gurobi's exact mixed-integer programming solver.

Capacitated Resource Allocation

Nov 2024 – Mar 2025 · CDC 2025
  • Designed a nonlinear optimization framework via the Maximum Entropy Principle and control barrier functions, guaranteeing KKT conditions while searching a large combinatorial space efficiently.
  • Achieved 71× and 4× speedups over SciPy's SLSQP and Safe Gradient Flow, with efficient constraint handling.

Dynamics and Controls · IIT Bombay & TU Munich, with Prof. Ravi N. Banavar and Dr. Paul Kotyczka

Geometric Modelling & Control of Flexible Link Manipulators

Jul 2021 – Jul 2022 · IFAC World Congress
  • Designed a control law for point-to-point manoeuvres of a flexible manipulator with suppressed flexural deflections.
  • Developed a Lie group variational integrator (LGVI) model of the manipulator and a preliminary convergence analysis for the evolution of beam cross-section rotations.

Coordinate-Invariant Modelling & Control of a 3-DOF Manipulator

Jul 2020 – Jun 2021 · LHMNC 2021
  • Developed a discrete-time, coordinate-free modelling and control algorithm for a 3-DOF revolute-joint manipulator, using geometric potential shaping for joint-angle control.
  • Validated the integrator's structure-preserving property in simulation, with average deviation on the order of 10−14 in all configuration variables.

Energy Optimisation in Wave Energy Converters

May 2019 – Mar 2020 · MED 2021
  • Conceptualized a WEC as a floating sphere with an internal 3D pendulum and derived and validated a dynamic model for 3D double-pendulum motion.
  • Formulated and solved an optimal control problem in CasADi–MATLAB to maximize energy conversion under limited actuation.
